Kokoro-82M on the Snapdragon NPU (optional download, Galaxy S23 only)

Model and voices: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (v1.0 ONNX, 28 English voices), Apache License 2.0.
  Split into an int8 ONNX CPU front end, CPU harmonic source and iSTFT, and Qualcomm HTP v73
  vocoder buckets with supplied per-voice AdaIN statistics. Downloaded bundle keeps LICENSE and NOTICE.

Misaki G2P (libmisaki_android.so): adapted from cedgeremek/kokoro-offline-tts-android (native/misaki-android)
  and hexgrad/misaki dictionaries, Apache License 2.0.

ONNX Runtime Android (com.microsoft.onnxruntime:onnxruntime-android 1.32.0): MIT License.

Kokoro NPU JNI runner (libkokoro_npu.so): QNN wrapper from gradium-ai/xn-ptts (MIT License)
  with Kokoro window streaming runner.

eSpeak NG 1.52.0 (libespeak-ng.so and espeak-ng-data/ in the downloaded bundle):
  GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (GPL-3.0-or-later).
  Used as an out-of-vocabulary English pronunciation fallback loaded dynamically by libmisaki_android.so.
  Source: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng (tag 1.52.0).
